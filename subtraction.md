#Understanding Integer Subtraction

Subtraction of integers means finding the difference between two values. On a number line, subtraction moves you to the **left**.

##Example: subtracting Two Integers
Let's subtract *5-3**.

### Step-by-step
1. Start ay **5** on the number line.
2. Move **3 steps to the left**.
3. You land of **2**.
5-3=2
